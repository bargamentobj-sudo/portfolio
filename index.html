CanteenIMS

Managing canteen inventory manually is a common challenge in many educational institutions and food service establishments. In most small canteen operations, stock tracking is done through handwritten records or simple spreadsheets, making it prone to human error, data inconsistency, and time-consuming reconciliation. The lack of a centralized and automated system results in inefficient stock monitoring, undetected shortages, and difficulty in maintaining accurate transaction logs.

My contribution
Front-end styling for the About page: the header and logo layout, the navigation bar with dropdown menus,and the responsive layout and backend for records and data management and admin panel

Sigbin Hunt

Sigbin Hunt is a 3D first-person horror investigation game in which the player is dispatched to investigate a string of missing livestock and unexplained disappearances in a rural barangay in Naga, Cebu. As the investigation unfolds across a farm, a forest trail, and finally the creature's lair, the player uncovers proof that a Sigbin a creature from Visayan folklore is responsible, and must gather materials, craft a weapon capable of harming it, corner and kill it, then escape the barangay before sunrise. The concept is inspired by a desire to build a horror game rooted in local Cebuano and Visayan culture, using the Sigbin's own folklore traits it’s a version to salt and garlic, its elusiveness as the foundation for the game's investigation, crafting, and confrontation mechanics, presented through a fully 3D, first-person perspective built in Roblox Studio for an immersive, accessible horror experience.

My contribution
Scripts game logic in Lua within Roblox Studio, including the Sigbin AI state machine, crafting system, QTE kill sequence, and cutscene triggers.
Built with
Roblox Studio
